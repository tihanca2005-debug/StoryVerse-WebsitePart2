About StoryVerse

StoryVerse was started in 2024 in Braamfontein with one simple idea - make good stories easy to find for movie and story lovers. We are a small independent bookstore and movie rental spot made for people who love to read and watch.

Our Mission

To create a welcoming space where every book and story lover can find their next favourite story without breaking the bank.

Where to find us

123 Jorissen Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg

We are open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm.
